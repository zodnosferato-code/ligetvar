# Rewritten Story and Plot Outline

> **Status:** 27 October 2026 rewrite based on playtest recommendations in story-coherence.md and explicit-scenes-teachable.md. | **Purpose:** Provide a coherent 16-week gothic romance plot that prioritizes lived relationships over bureaucratic investigation, with late Soma reveal, and toned-down implied intimacy ready for later explicit insertion.

## Intimacy authoring note

Sexual content remains part of the relationships. Acts occur (kisses escalating to undressing, mutual touch, oral, penetration, etc., per character paces), but language is toned down. Scenes fade to black after mutual affirmative choice and invitation. Aftermath shows care, conversation, and changed closeness. Explicit physical descriptions are omitted here; later a dedicated pass can insert full graphic scenes into the fade points without changing the emotional beats, choices, or plot. Refusal, slowing, and reconsideration always available and acknowledged. Intimacy does not gate endings or investigation.

## Core plot changes from recommendations

- Soma remains a brief, ordinary background assistant until weeks 13–14. Sparse retrospective clues (access timing, familiar objects). No early identification, confession, or five weeks of passive waiting.
- Shared mid-semester scenes change personal or practical situations (workshop magic surprise, exhausted return prompting company, quiet recovery) rather than successive document verifications and speeches.
- Relationship commitments and triad evening open earlier for lived time before climax.
- Distinct attraction sources, complications, and changes per woman.
- Lightly magical workshop moments for chemistry (shared sensory impressions, noticed tension); magic never creates affection.
- Explicitly framed Rebeka–Boglárka interludes; knowledge only via what they share.
- Distinct institutional outcomes: Virág’s corruption report succeeds independently; college response to supernatural depends on evidence survival and disclosure.

Canon retained: deliberate transformation, fascination without compelled affection, returning exhausted victims, genuine mystical gymnastics, death in confrontation, institutional/personal aftermath. Romance-first: story is the setting for dating.

## Sixteen-week rewritten outline

### Weeks 1–3: Settling in and first sparks

Márton opens the mystical gymnastics workshop. The women attend for their own reasons and claim the space. Practice yields small magical effects: a shared grounding produces a fleeting mutual sensory impression of warmth or sound, prompting laughter and lingering. Personal invitations arise from curiosity.

Lilla returns exhausted from a missing evening, choosing the chair by the door; the group spends time with her rather than extracting a report. Soma appears briefly as an ordinary assistant fetching supplies. Virág flirts directly in the hallway, establishing adult status; an early private encounter is available after mutual yes (fade after confirmation of desire to continue privately).

### Weeks 4–6: Attraction and first shadows

Favourite places and early dates establish distinct feelings. Rebeka challenges polished images through photography choices in the screening room. Boglárka shares quiet pleasure in the library alcove, putting books aside. Gréta finds space outside usefulness at the lakeside. Virág’s instrumental motives surface with accountability; she keeps teasing after refusals.

A repeated voice in an empty room feels uncanny; a grounding exercise reveals the unnatural repetition. A second exhausted return ends with changed evening plans and seeking company, not another completed checklist. Light magic helps notice tension or a missing detail, creating excuses to stay together.

Implied intimacy begins in private dates: after invitation and mutual choice (slow or continue), fade during undressing or first touch; return to conversation about staying without performance or usefulness.

### Weeks 7–9: Ordinary life with gaps

Relationships lead. Unease via missing time and subtly wrong familiar spaces. Workshop moments remain lightly magical and relational. Soma stays peripheral with mundane reasons to appear; no villain framing.

Rebeka and Boglárka develop independently (clearly framed interludes if retained; Márton learns only what they choose to share). Commitments can begin opening here for breathing room.

### Weeks 10–12: Closeness without certainty

Dates and personal complications foreground. Group practices protection without knowing the source. Virág pursues after-hours records room searches independently. Retrospective clues plant (ordinary access logs or objects that later connect).

Implied sexual acts continue per character: Rebeka choosing not to hide her scar in aftermath; Boglárka choosing quiet pleasure over performance; Gréta leaving the phone in the bag so the moment is not work. Fade after yes; aftermath advances closeness.

### Weeks 13–14: Commitments and late reveal

Key decisions breathe: individual commitments and the triad ordinary evening occur with room for lived relationship, not last-minute unlock. Preparation is concise and practical, affecting intervention cost only.

A small set of prior details now identifies Soma. Motive and deliberate transformation become clear near the confrontation. Warmth, humor, and voluntary closeness alongside dread. Virág’s reporting succeeds independently.

### Week 15: Confrontation

Late discovery leads promptly to intervention in the service chamber. Workshop experiences and preparation have payoffs. Soma dies when the unstable transformation is interrupted. Immediate aftermath reflects exhaustion and relief; later meetings acknowledge it.

### Week 16: Aftermath and futures

Institutional response distinguished from Virág’s verified corruption findings. Personal follow-ups show concrete continuation, friendship, or withdrawal. Favourite places revisited with shared memories.
