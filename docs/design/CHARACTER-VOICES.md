## Natural conversation — user direction, 28 October 2026

Remove repetitive permission asking and abstract reassurance speeches during the scene-by-scene rewrite. Let people answer questions directly, make ordinary offers, tease, hesitate and act. One stated preference followed in action is stronger than several characters repeating its principle. Consequential invitations and refusals remain meaningful choices; express them in each character's own voice, without a formal approval exchange for every harmless step. This is a writing direction, not a bulk mechanical replacement of phrases across unreviewed scenes.

## Expanded character profiles

### Rebeka Dorn
**What she does:** Documents the workshop and castle through photography. Challenges polished images and institutional narratives. Prefers choosing what is seen over performing for approval.

**What she likes about Márton:** His willingness to be challenged and not hide behind authority. She enjoys when he drops the teacher role and lets her set the frame (literally and figuratively). His competence without needing to dominate appeals to her.

**Sexual fantasies (implied):** Being seen and chosen without needing to perform or entertain. She fantasizes about private moments where she can drop the sarcasm and simply be wanted — slow undressing, being looked at with attention rather than spectacle, and staying afterward without needing a joke. Mutual choice to continue (kiss to deeper touch) is key; she likes control over the pace and what remains private.

**Favourite location — Holdkör screening room:**
- Choose enjoyably terrible films together and mock them.
- Improvise miniature shadow scenes against the screen.
- Trade photographs of overlooked castle details.
- Linger while the projector cools after everyone leaves — quiet conversation that can turn intimate after mutual yes (fade during escalation).
Common activities with the player: screening nights, photo walks of castle corners, deciding what to share publicly vs. keep private.

### Boglárka Fazekas
**What she does:** Studies folklore and sources carefully. Turns research into something with her own name and voice. Prefers quiet company over performed scholarship.

**What she likes about Márton:** That he listens and does not interrupt or fill silence with noise. She likes when he notices small details she cares about and gives her space to choose pleasure rather than duty.

**Sexual fantasies (implied):** Quiet, deliberate pleasure chosen for its own sake. She fantasizes about being touched slowly after she asks, mutual oral attention without performance, and returning to a shared quiet afterward (books set aside, rain on the window). She enjoys praise of what she chooses to show, not generic compliments.

**Favourite location — Library window alcove:**
- Read competing passages aloud in whispers.
- Exchange handwritten bookmarks and invent lives for marginal notes.
- Listen to rain together after deliberately putting the books away.
- Soft conversation that can lead to closeness after clear mutual choice (fade).
Common activities with the player: shared reading, bookmark trades, rainy afternoons, choosing quiet over productivity.

### Gréta Váradi
**What she does:** Organizes the workshop’s academic administration and keeps schedules reliable. She wants recognition for judgment, not just usefulness.

**What she likes about Márton:** That he can leave work unfinished and still want her company. She likes when he treats time with her as not another task to manage.

**Sexual fantasies (implied):** Pleasure that exists outside usefulness and organization. She fantasizes about leaving the phone in the bag, being wanted at a table or outdoors without it becoming “minutes,” light playful escalation after she confirms she wants it, and laughing afterward. Position and pace checked explicitly; aftermath keeps the moment free of work.

**Favourite location — Lakeside willow and walking trail:**
- Take unplanned detours.
- Skim stones with an absurd private scoring rule.
- Share an imperfect picnic.
- Shelter from rain and decide whether to wait or walk back laughing.
- Quiet moments that can turn intimate after mutual yes (fade).
Common activities with the player: walking, picnics, stone-skimming games, escaping schedules together.

### Virág
**What she does:** Investigates university corruption as a junior reporter for Mérceháló. Uses a college-inspired look to pass as a first-year. Initiates sexual closeness from the first meeting for both practical help and her own amusement.

**What she likes about Márton:** That he is a useful insider who can also match her directness. She enjoys the tension of seducing him while knowing he can refuse; his competence and willingness to play along (or challenge her) amuse her. Real attraction grows alongside the instrumental motive.

**Sexual fantasies (implied):** Immediate and repeated initiation. She fantasizes about quick private encounters (kiss escalating to undressing and mutual acts after a clear yes), teasing him into closeness in hallways, lakesides, or after-hours searches, and continuing to initiate even after refusals. Acts are implied via fade-to-black; she keeps the teasing personality either way.

**Favourite location — School after hours (old administrative records room):**
- Search for missing annexes and compare scholarship/renovation records by lamplight.
- Exchange whispered jokes when footsteps pass.
- Take a quiet break after a discovery.
- Sexual initiation possible during or after the search once mutual choice is confirmed (fade).
Common activities with the player: night document searches, comparing records, whispered banter, decompressing together, early and repeated private encounters.
