### Writing strengths to retain

The expanded opening uses specific objects and reactions: the cable, crooked circle, marked photograph, the word “perhaps,” and Gréta's map. These details produce personality without explaining it. Lilla choosing the chair by the door is stronger than another speech about respecting choices. Preserve that technique.

The chief prose problem is repetition of principles as declarations. Boundaries, independent choice and evidence quality matter, but having several characters announce them in successive scenes flattens voices. State an agreement when needed; later show characters keeping, misunderstanding or renegotiating it. Consent remains explicit at consequential moments.

## Length assessment

Sixteen weeks provide 16 compulsory shared activities and 32 discretionary activities: 48 activity completions, not 48 equally long scenes.

A single main romance requires six individual scenes. A triad continuation uses ten individual pre-confrontation scenes, T1–T7, and T8: 18 discretionary scenes; separate individual aftermath scenes are not required because T8 supplies both aftermath decisions. Eight Virág chapters plus two preparation tasks bring this combined target to 28 of 32 discretionary slots, leaving four for jobs, outings, coffee, rest or other meetings. This is a count, not a proof of schedule feasibility. Day/night restrictions and narrow windows matter more than the total.

**Recommendation:** retain 16 weeks. More weeks would not fix the stalled investigation or late gate congestion. A single route needs more varied optional relationship experiences; a triad-plus-Virág run needs scheduling flexibility. Do not require seeing all optional content in one playthrough. No defensible hours estimate exists without counting reachable reading paths and timing play.

## Revised English story outline

This is the maintained story-level rewrite incorporating playtest recommendations. Existing Hungarian dialogue, choices, journal text and runtime endings stay unchanged for the separate localization/conversation refactor. The detailed beats below are editorial proposals within the confirmed direction; they are not claims of shipped scenes or approval of new supernatural mechanics.

### Weeks 1–3: reasons to return

Márton opens a magical movement workshop at the castle college. The women attend for different reasons and quickly make the room their own. Shared practice provides playful friction, small surprises and excuses to spend time together. Lightly magical moments (shared sensory impressions, noticed tension) enable chemistry. Personal invitations emerge from curiosity rather than rewards for investigation.

Lilla's missing evening introduces unease into a place the player is beginning to enjoy. She returns as a person with her own preferences, not a clue delivery device. Soma appears briefly in an ordinary assistant role. Avoid ominous exits, conspicuous resentment or repeated attention to his sudden success. Any small irregularity should make sense as ordinary college life on first encounter and acquire meaning only later.

Virág enters through her established separate storyline. She is sexually active and seductive from the first meeting, pushing for acts (implied fade-to-black after mutual choice) for help and amusement. Her direct pace contrasts with the other women without replacing their relationships.

### Weeks 4–6: attraction under a shadow

Regular workshop life and early dates establish what each relationship feels like when nobody is solving a crisis. Rebeka challenges the image Márton presents; Boglárka invites attention toward something she chose; Gréta creates room for pleasure outside being useful. Virág's openness comes with her own instrumental motives and accountability; she continues initiating sexual closeness.

The repeated voice turns an ordinary shared place uncanny. A second exhausted return establishes danger, but the shared episode should end in a changed feeling or practical situation, not another completed report. Quiet recovery gives the player a reason to seek company.

### Weeks 7–9: ordinary life with unexplained gaps

Relationships and favourite places lead the semester. Unease appears through missing time, familiar spaces feeling subtly wrong, and returned people whose experiences do not yet identify a culprit. Workshop learning and mutual care provide useful responses without turning every shared meeting into an investigation briefing.

Soma remains an occasional background assistant with mundane reasons to be present. Do not stage a confession, villain speech, repeated suspicious offer or supervised test of his powers. Establish the phenomenon's limits through observations and sources without requiring a known vampire to demonstrate them.

Rebeka and Boglárka's relationship develops independently. If interludes are retained, introduce them clearly rather than pretending Márton witnessed them.

### Weeks 10–12: closeness before certainty

Dates, personal complications and college events remain the foreground. The group learns enough about the supernatural danger to protect people and practise together without yet knowing who caused it. Virág follows her separate corruption leads during after-hours school searches; she does not become the mechanism that exposes Soma. She continues sexual initiation during or after these.

Plant a few ordinary, retrospectively meaningful details involving access, timing or familiar objects. Each must have a fair initial interpretation. Avoid both an obvious culprit spotlight and an unprepared surprise. The specific clues and magical rules require the later script pass.

The proposed outline places firm identification in weeks 13–14, shortly before the week-15 confrontation. Exact timing is not user-confirmed. Earlier preparation concerns the danger and group safety rather than a five-week campaign against an already confessed villain.

### Weeks 13–14: chosen commitments, ordinary pleasure

Bring key relationship decisions forward enough to breathe. A commitment should create space for a relationship to be lived, not simply unlock an ending just before the boss scene. Give the triad its ordinary evening without making the player solve a hidden scheduling puzzle.

A small set of previously established details now connects Soma to the harm. The revelation reinterprets a familiar background presence; it does not introduce a new villain or depend on romance access. His motive becomes clear near the confrontation rather than through a long sequence of personal appearances.

Preparation is concise and practical. It affects the cost of intervention, never eligibility for affection. These weeks need warmth, humor and voluntary closeness alongside dread. Preserve Virág's independent casual-contact direction and successful reporting outcome. Sexual acts remain implied.

### Week 15: intervention and its immediate aftermath

The late discovery leads promptly to intervention. Soma's motive and deliberate transformation are established without a prolonged confession scene or a last-minute new magical weakness. Earlier workshop experiences and preparation have visible payoffs. Soma dies when the transformation is interrupted, as established canon requires. The scene's tension comes from carrying out a dangerous action and getting people out, not a late new weakness.

Make the aftermath immediate enough that a later meeting does not behave as if nothing happened. Dating remains possible, but exhaustion, relief or a wish for quiet should inform its framing. The current shared script's identical branches and flag-driven outcome need a later conversation pass.

### Week 16: relationships beyond the semester

Resolve institutional responsibility before the personal follow-ups. Clearly separate Virág's corruption findings from the supernatural case. Show what the institution's response changes for the affected people.

Personal scenes answer whether and how each chosen relationship continues. Use concrete future plans and character-specific gestures, not repeated declarations that nobody owes affection. Friendship, withdrawal and uncertainty remain valid outcomes. Preserve the two discretionary slots and the triad's consolidated aftermath.
