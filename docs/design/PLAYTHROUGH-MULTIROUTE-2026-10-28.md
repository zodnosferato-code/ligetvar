## Strengths to preserve

- **Opening ensemble:** shared_1/opening_questions_0–4 and shared_2/missing_inventory_0–5 turn chairs and photographs into distinct voices. The women respond to one another, rather than waiting for player reassurance.
- **Lilla's ordinary presence:** shared_3/interview_listen_0–4 and interview_finish_0–4 give her library annoyance, humor, a preferred seat and coffee. Keep this alongside her right to decide what she shares.
- **Boglárka's specificity:** boglarka_1/boglarka_versions_0–6 makes self-authorship visible through deleting “sorry.” Her tea invitation in boglarka_2 is warmer than an abstract agency speech.
- **Gréta's ordinary wants:** greta_1/greta_map_2–4 and greta_2/romance_attraction_ordinary_0–3 make the duck/shoe jokes feel like her, while expressing a wish to be wanted outside work.
- **Rebeka's vulnerability:** rebeka_2/romance_attraction_honest_0–4 and rebeka_4/romance_repair_ask_space_0–2 allow embarrassment and ongoing anger. Do not resolve every disagreement instantly.
- **Independent Rebeka–Boglárka connection:** triad_3/romance_rb_private_0–8 and triad_4/romance_rb_kiss_0–7 give them attention independent of the protagonist. Triad_7's paprika dinner is worth retaining when removing its automatic escalation.
- **Functional alternatives:** slower Rebeka still ended in romance; late withdrawal ended estranged; friendship still resolved the vampire threat. All eight Virág refusal chapters completed, with her reporting success intact. These are valuable foundations, not proof every branch is already well written.
