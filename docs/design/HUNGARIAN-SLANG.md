## Tone guidance
- Main romance routes (Rebeka, Boglárka, Gréta): character-specific register. Rebeka more controlled/direct; Boglárka quieter and precise; Gréta practical and energetic. Prefer natural spoken phrasing over clinical or purely soft wording.

- Avoid ritual permission lectures, childish euphemisms, and purely off-screen softening in explicit branches.
- Prefer terms still current in young-adult and mature Hungarian speech.

## Usage rules
- Match the speaking character’s established voice. Do not force the same vulgarity level on every route.
- English remains the canonical source; Hungarian is the translation. When English is revised, re-check the Hungarian against this list.
- Document any new preferred term here when it is first used in a scene.
- Optional content never gates the investigation or endings.
